About Treats By Rich

Luxury dessert craftsmanship rooted in warmth and freshness

Treats By Rich began as a celebration of premium ingredients, polished presentation and the pleasure of sharing something beautiful. Each parfait is designed to feel indulgent, balanced and memorable.

Our philosophy

We believe that premium dessert should feel uplifting, thoughtful and easy to enjoy. Every cup is assembled with fresh fruit, natural yogurt and carefully selected toppings for the perfect balance of texture and flavor.

What makes us different

From the way the layers are arranged to the way our menu is curated, everything is designed to feel elevated without losing the warmth and energy of a true neighborhood dessert house.

Fresh by design

Beautiful presentation
